## Gotchas

- The binder is only rendered while its tab is shown (`renderBinder` marks itself dirty otherwise) and grids build in chunks of 24 to 60 cards, then a few hundred in idle time, then on scroll. Keep it that way: 3000 cards built at once took tens of seconds. `tests/suites/perf.mjs` guards it.
- `main` is live. Run `npm run check` and `node tests/run.mjs` before pushing; push to a branch first if unsure.
- Testing levels: stubs first (`node tests/run.mjs`), then the local Supabase stack for anything server side, then the live game with a dedicated test account. Nothing destructive on the live database, and never Gabriel's own account, without asking him.
- Tests that wait on the cloud save must call `window.__wikster.flushSync()` instead of sleeping; the sync is batched.
- A promise rejected with "Target page, context or browser has been closed" at the end of a suite is ignored by `tests/lib/guard.mjs`; any other error still fails.
- Card art comes from upload.wikimedia.org; Wikipedia needs the Wikster User-Agent on the server side.
- The `.screen:not(.is-active) *` rule pauses every animation on hidden screens; render a screen's content before or after `showScreen`, it resumes.
- Writing a save key that does not change is a no-op on purpose (`writeJson` compares first).

## The economy lives on the server

- Coins, Ink, boosters, cards and most profile progress are owned by the `economy` edge function. `src/econ/engine.js` is bundled for the server by `tools/build-economy.mjs` into `supabase/functions/economy/engine.js` plus lazy `engine-*.js` chunks (and `billing/catalog.js`); all gitignored. The build splits the Wikdle word lists into chunks loaded on the first `wikdle` call, keeps only the English strings the server's `t()` calls name (it fails if one is missing, keeps everything if a key is computed) and drops French (engine.js 648 KB to 374 KB). It also rewrites the chunk imports with spaces, because `supabase functions deploy --use-api` misses minified `import{..}from"..."` and the deploy then fails with "Module not found".
- The client predicts most actions locally with the same engine (`src/app/econ.js`, `src/econ/local.js`) and confirms in the background. `SERVER_ONLY` actions wait for the server.
- Server replies mirror state into the local profile (`ECON_KEYS` in `src/econ/core.js`). A key the server leaves out is deleted locally, so `store.normalizeProfile` restores defaults after every apply. Forgetting this blanked the shop once.
- Packs are drawn ahead of time and opening never waits on the network (`src/app/ready.js`). `ready` answers at once with the draws already waiting and tops up in the background (`EdgeRuntime.waitUntil`). The reserve is sized to the boosters held (`reservePlan` in `src/econ/rules.js`, shared by the phone and the server: every booster held up to 5 per kind and 15 in all, at least 3 per kind and 5 for the one on screen first). The server draws many boosters of a kind in one go (`drawPulls` in the engine, `drawArticlesMany` in `src/wiki/core.js`: pooled Wikipedia searches or one Fandom gathering, page views read in parallel, one bulk insert with `stashMany`), and sends the draws in `ready` events of 8. Draws come from article pools when they can (`src/wiki/pool.js`, tables `wiki_pools` and `wiki_pool`): one bounded pool per Wikipedia theme (by its queries), custom wiki (shared between players), Today day and title list, 320 compact rows for small sources, 600 for a Wikipedia theme and up to 1200 for a wiki with thousands of pages (15% of its articles), 20k in all (least recently used pools evicted, idle custom pools first). A draw is one indexed `wiki_pool_draw` read that hands rows in random order, the player's unseen ones first: articles they own or hold in a waiting draw come last (`fresh` counts the others), so a player sees a whole pool before any repeat, with no state kept for it. When a full pool is used up for a player the rest is drawn live and added to the pool. A pool also rotates once its draws pass half its size (counted in memory, `p_rotate`); the cards are then shuffled with `ctx.random` and dealt with the same rarity roll as a live draw (`dealSets`), so hit slot, pity and prints work as before. A pool is refilled in the background (`waitUntil`) only when it is low or older than 6 hours, with a lock in `wiki_pools.refill_after` (10 minutes after a refill, 6 hours when the source gave little new), at most 2 refills per function instance, backoff after a failure, and one `wiki_pool_fill` write per refill. An empty pool falls back to a live draw. Any action that gains boosters without a `ready` ask starts the fill itself (`kickFill`, one per player at a time, 5 s apart). `prepareMany` (up to 60) answers from the pool at once and streams what the pool could not cover in `ready` events (`filling`), and `ready` waits up to 1.5 s for a pool-backed fill so its reply already holds the draws. Economy replies that change boosters carry the fresh draws (`readyNow`), and a finished fill sends a `ready` event on `user:<id>`, so the phone only calls `ready` itself when it is short and nothing is drawing, or when the live feed is down. `ready` writes no `rate_counters` row. Draws are kept on the device (`wikster.ready.v1`, 48 max) and their pictures decoded in `src/app/pictures.js`, 6 at a time, the booster on screen first. Opening never waits for a picture: the card shows at once and its picture fades in.
- An opening is written to a journal (`wikster.opening.v1`) before the reveal and confirmed later on its own channel, in order, with retries. `open` is idempotent (a nonce already claimed answers `outcome: 'already'` with its cards) and claims by nonce, not in order. Server replies are overlaid with the pending openings (`useServerOverlay`), so a booster never comes back and a card never vanishes. When no draw is ready the booster still opens: its cards wait face down as "Cards arriving" until the draw lands (offline too, it resolves on `online`). Open all reveals the draws already on the device at once and asks for all the missing ones in one `prepareMany`, showing "12 / 30 boosters ready" while the rest land booster by booster, in order; the journal entry is held (`hold`, grown with `growOpen`) until they land, so Open all of any size is at most two requests (`prepareMany` and one `open`). XP, level ups, quest, season and album toasts wait until the last card is turned or the summary shows (`src/app/hush.js`, `afterReveal`), so nothing spoils the pull; the economy itself is applied at once. Timed open-all merges single timed draws (`mergedNonce(parts)`, merged on the server at `open`).
- Booster rules live in `src/econ/rules.js`, shared by the server and the phone: the last card of a booster of 3 or more is Uncommon or better (hit slot, not for code or `exact` specs), pity guarantees a Legendary after `pity.legendary` (tuning, 40) boosters without one (`state.pity`, last opens in `state.opens`), and every copy keeps its print (`cards.prints` `{rarity: count}`, best print shown, spares used first, rows without prints are one rarity).
- One action costs one `econ_load` (it also returns the cards the action needs and the pull with its cards) and one `econ_apply`. Card totals live on the `econ` row (`n_cards`, `n_unique`, `n_value`), so profile stats never re-sum the collection. `commit()` skips the write when nothing changes.
- Confirmations time out at 10 s (non-predicted calls 25 s). Predicted actions waiting behind a slow answer go as one `batch` request. Startup sends `since` and gets only cards changed since then plus `cards_gone`.
- State deltas (`src/econ/delta.js`): every economy call sends `sv`, the version (hash of the sorted JSON) of the last server state and inventory the phone holds. When it matches what the server loaded first, the reply carries `delta: { state, inventory }` (`set` and `gone` keys) instead of the whole document, plus the new `sv`. `ask()` in `src/app/econ.js` mends it before anything else sees it and checks the hash; a mismatch drops the base and asks for a snapshot. No `sv` (old apps) means the full reply as before, and a version the server cannot match gets the full document. The base lives in memory and is saved to `wikster.econBase.v1` on hide. `tests/unit/delta.test.mjs` plays a game both ways and compares.
- Retries (`src/backoff.js`): jittered exponential backoff (1 s doubling, 30 s cap), Retry-After honoured (the economy 429 sends it, exposed through CORS), paused while offline, resumed on `online` and on return. A request is sent again only when it never landed (429, 503, made offline) or the action is safe to repeat (`resendSafe` in `src/app/econ.js`: claim keys, nonces, `req` ids, reads). Anything else that failed in an unknown way (timeout, 5xx, dropped connection) is settled by a snapshot, never resent: `sell`, `fuse`, `atelier`, `exchange`, `timed`, arcade and quiz plays would apply twice. A replay refused after an unknown failure counts as landed and triggers a snapshot. The cloud sync retries from 5 s up to 2 minutes; the opening journal and `ready` use the same backoff.
- Reply order (`src/app/econ.js`): every economy reply is stamped with when it was sent and received and its `state.rev`. A reply that is provably older than the last one that wrote a card (received before that one was sent, or overlapping with a lower `rev`) never overwrites that card, and an older state, wallet or inventory is skipped. Before this, a held predicted reply applied at drain could delete a card a newer opening reply had just given. Replies also carry `totals` (`n_cards`, `n_unique` from `econ_load` / `econ_apply`); when nothing is in flight and no opening is pending, a mismatch with the local collection asks for one quiet full snapshot (at most every 2 minutes, given up for the session if a snapshot does not fix it).
- A failed predicted action or an unknown failure (timeout, 5xx) of a non repeatable one is settled by one quiet snapshot (`settleSoon`, backoff, 6 tries), never resent. `economyIdle()` always resolves, even when the drain starts a follow-up request. Outage toasts (slow, server down, busy, offline, slow down) show once per outage: again only after 60 s or a reply that came back within 4 s (`useToastGate` in `core.js`). `askHouse` aborts every request at its timeout.
- Leaving the app: on hide and `pagehide`, `leaving()` (`src/account/client.js`) makes every Supabase request keepalive for 15 s (up to 60 KB in flight, larger bodies go as plain fetch), queued predicted actions leave at once as one batch (`flushEconomy`), the journal confirms, and the sync runs even from the opening screen.
- The Supabase gateway already gzips function replies (`Content-Encoding: gzip`, `Vary: Accept-Encoding`, checked on staging), so the functions do not compress themselves.
- Release order: run `schema.sql` before the functions deploy, or `index.ts` falls back to the slow legacy loader.
- Control's grants go through the `grants` table and are claimed by the server `grants` action; `profile` and `profileAdd` rows the server cannot own are applied on the device.
- Every reward writes a claim key (`quest:<day>:<id>`, `daily:<day>`, `ach:<id>`, `rung:<season>:<i>`, `stipendh:<hour>`...) in `claims`; a second insert raises `ALREADY_CLAIMED`. Claimed daily quests are also kept in the econ state (`questDay`), and in server mode the quest board shows claimed from that, never from the device alone.
- The shop restocks every hour (`REFRESH_MS`); the stipend is 250 per hour and banks 8 hours. Old saves carry a two hour `stipendWindow`, read through `stipendHourOf`.
- Profile Statistics (`src/profilestats.js` computes, `src/app/statsboard.js` paints, memoised on `saveWrites()`): everything comes from loaded state, no request. `state.pullStats` (`notePulls` in `src/econ/rules.js`, written by `open` in the same commit) keeps boosters and value since it started, today, the last 12 weeks, the luckiest day and the best pull; `daily` keeps `run`, `best` and `total`. Both count from October 2026, so older players see them fill up. The board is public: `toSummary` packs the shown numbers and week series (no card keys or titles, under 1.5 KB) into `profiles.stats`, sent inside `sync_me` (`p_stats.summary`) when it changed, at most every 15 minutes or on leaving, cleaned by `stats_clean` (known keys, numbers only, capped) in `sync_me` and the `profile_stats_guard` trigger. Viewers read it with the profile row (`profileStats`) and `fromSummary` rebuilds the board; trusted columns (cards, unique, value, boosters, play time) win. Settings `publicStats` off sends `{off:1}` and viewers see only the old columns. Profiles without a summary still show the old cells.
- "Claim all" buttons (`src/app/claimall.js`) send their claims with `gather`, so the predicted actions leave in one `batch` request.

## Custom boosters, pictures and mature wikis

- The finder (`src/wiki/finder.js`) searches Fandom (server only, its search has no CORS), Wikidata wiki ids (Fandom, wiki.gg, Miraheze, official wiki), Miraheze, slug guesses, the known list in `src/data/wikis.js` and a Wikipedia topic. Every candidate is probed with siteinfo and `rankResults` picks, in order: the player's language, not fan made (`offCanon`: fanon, fanfiction, roleplay, ideas, non-canon, homebrew...), an exact name over a near one, a known wiki, then the most pages (Wikidata listed ones count 1.5x); mature wikis are never the default pick unless the query itself is mature and the player opted in. The server action `wikiFind` caches searches in `wiki_finds` (keys carry `FINDER_VERSION`, bump it when the ranking changes) and probes in `wiki_sites`; the phone falls back to its own finder. The "Biggest" tag in the creator goes to the largest real page count shown (`biggestIndex`), never to a Wikipedia topic. `inspectWiki` (server customSave) also samples categories (`matureContent`) and stores the result in `wiki_sites`. Some hosts answer 403 to the Supabase edge, so their draws fail with `WIKI_DOWN`.
- Errors: `src/house.js` turns a 5xx without a code into `SERVER_DOWN`; `customSave`, `customDelete` and `wikiFind` are retried twice (`askAgain` in `src/app/econ.js`); every code has a message in `MESSAGES` (per action in `ACTION_MESSAGES`), unknown and server codes are reported once per session to `client_errors` (kind `econ`). Draw failures on the server are `WIKI_DOWN` or `DRAW_FAILED` with a `detail`, never a raw 500.
- Custom draws read Fandom leads in two batch requests (revisions, then one `parse` POST), and old wikis' pictures through `prop=images`. A Wikipedia topic booster draws from a search for its topic.
- Pictures (`src/wiki/art.js`): page image (PageImages), then for pages without one, in one batched pass per 20 titles: the page's own files (`prop=images` listing, which also brings the Wikidata id, then `imageinfo`; a file must be named after the page, `fileScore`, icons, flags, logos and maps only when they carry the whole title), Wikidata, closest linked page, Openverse, then a designed text card. Many wiki.gg, Miraheze and self hosted wikis have no PageImages, so the own files step is what gives their item sprites. Small png/gif sprites (under 128 px) get `picture.pixel` and a `#wkpx` mark on the URL; the phone draws them crisp and centered (`is-pixel`, `img[src$="#wkpx"]` in `cards.css`). Kept per host and title in `card_pictures` (source `page` for own files, needs the current `schema.sql`); a text result is searched again after 3 days or when `PICTURE_VERSION` changes. Requests go through `fetchJsonRetry` because wiki.gg answers 429 with Retry-After across all its wikis. A text result is not kept when the file listing failed (a busy wiki is asked again next time). Openverse photos must match every word of the title, and a one word title must also match the booster name. Wikis whose `extracts` answer slowly (Terraria takes 5 to 12 s for 20 pages) are remembered in `slowExtracts` and read without extracts, their leads come from the batched revisions and `parse` path. Cards carry `picture` for the credit line.
- Stored cards with a text card are upgraded by the server on the snapshot (`src/econ/picfix.js`, `fixCardPictures`): up to 24 per launch, at most every 6 hours per player, one batched `findPictures` per wiki, each card stamped `pictureCheck` so it is looked at once per `PICTURE_VERSION`.
- Pools keep a text card as `picture: { source: 'text' }` with no drawing (the drawing is remade from the title at deal time), deal pictured articles first, and a refill that is due anyway looks again for the pictures of the text cards it just sampled (`repicture` in `src/wiki/pool.js`). Custom pool ids carry `CUSTOM_POOL_VERSION`; bump it when what a custom draw keeps changes. Custom pools sample twice what a batch needs (`CUSTOM_SAMPLE_SLACK`) so text cards stay out when the pool has pictured ones. Cards taken from the older `draw_pool` stock go through `stockable` (same title, picture, lead and topic rules).
- Custom draws keep real articles only (`protoCard` / `junkTitle` / `offTopic` in `src/wiki/custom.js`): no disambiguation (`pageprops.disambiguation`), no subpages on wikis other than Wikipedia, no version, changelog, file-like or namespace titles, no meta pages (policy, help, guides, lists, templates... by title or by a whole category name), at least 100 characters of lead, and on mod wikis (sitename says mod, expansion, overhaul) no base game pages (lead says "is a vanilla", vanilla categories). Wikipedia theme draws drop disambiguation pages too.
- Mature: `user_metadata.age_18_plus` and `mature_wikis` (`src/age.js`). Wikipedia topic boosters about sexual anatomy, sexuality or sexual content are mature too (`matureTopic` in `src/wiki/mature.js`), and cards whose title or description names sexual anatomy are sensitive (`sexualName` in `src/sensitive.js`). The `custom_packs` name trigger screens a mature booster with the `adultPack` scope (no sexual tier), like the engine. The server refuses saving, buying and drawing mature boosters otherwise (`MATURE_LOCKED`). `html[data-mature-lock='1']` blurs mature cards with no reveal.
- Some mature wiki rules are creator decisions kept in CLAUDE.local.md (not in the repo). Read it before touching `src/wiki/safety.js` or the mature lists in `src/data/wikis.js`. The list of adult wikis the finder may suggest is not in the code: it is the server-only table `adult_wikis` (no policy, read by the economy function through `useAdultWikis` in `src/wiki/finder.js`, cached 10 minutes); the phone's own fallback finder has none. Tests use `ADULT_FIXTURE` from `tests/lib/codefixtures.mjs`.
- Minors: `minorsText` / `minorsWiki` / `minorsCard` in `src/wiki/safety.js` refuse loli, shota, jailbait, underage, child porn and the like in several languages and leet spellings, always, and words such as cub, teen, kids, enfants when the context is adult. Server and phone both refuse (`CONTENT_REFUSED`), the finder answers empty, `isUsableText` drops such pages and every draw is filtered. Never relax this.
- No NSFW content (Settings, `noNsfwRow` in `src/app/mature.js`): `user_metadata.no_nsfw` for accounts (the profile setting `noNsfw` for guests, pushed to the account once on sign in), overrides the mature opt in. The server draws with `pack.safe` and filters every booster through `cardAllowed` (mature and `isSensitive` cards out, topped up from another draw); turning it on calls `safeReady`, which drops waiting pulls holding mature cards, and the phone drops them from its ready cache. Mature cards are hidden by CSS wherever they carry `data-adult` (binder, market, trades, showcases, pickers), except on the opening screen.
- Title draws (code boosters, theme title rolls, Wikipedia Today) resolve every title of the booster in one batched query with retries and Retry-After (`titleCards` in `src/wiki/translate.js`, `fetchJsonRetry` in `src/wiki/core.js`). If Wikipedia keeps failing the draw fails and the booster stays sealed; only a page that does not exist becomes a designed card. Placeholders stored before that are repaired by the server on the next snapshot, once per player (`src/econ/fix.js`, `state.cardFix`), rekeyed to the page id key with copies, prints and favorite, and the reply carries `renamed` so the phone moves showcase pins.
- Special card photos (the code boosters' extra cards) are never in the repo: they live in the `friend-pictures` bucket and their URL is in each code's definition (`extra.photo`). `withSpecialPhoto` / `specialPhoto` in `src/codedefs.js` put it on the card, and `src/econ/fix.js` rewrites stored cards that still carry an old address.
- Custom boosters in the shop take a quantity (`CUSTOM_QTY_RANGE`, 1 to 10) under the size: `buy` with `count` and a `req` id (claim `buy:<req>`, a replay answers `already`). Size and quantity are kept per wiki on the device in `wikster.customBuy.v1`.

## Saves and load on the server

- Every save key write stamps it (`src/save.js`). Only real save keys trigger a cloud sync, and only when the value changed. Keys the server owns when the economy is live (collection, wallet, ink, inventory, customPacks: `serverOwnedKeys` in `src/app/econ.js`) never trigger a sync and are left out of the cloud save.
- For live players the cloud save is one row per key in `save_keys` (profile, language, ripDirection, theme), written by the `sync_me` RPC in one round trip together with play time, rank, badges, showcase and last seen. The profile sent leaves out the server-owned fields and floors play time to 15 minutes (`src/savekeys.js`). Players who are not live still use the old `saves` blob, and old apps' blob writes reach `save_keys` through the `saves_to_keys` trigger.
- Sync is batched in `src/app/gate.js`: 20 s after the last change, at most 60 s after the first, and on pagehide / hidden. With nothing dirty no call is made. Backups go to `saves_history` at most every 10 minutes. `friend_cards` reads a live player's cards from `cards`, not from the blob.
- Launch is local first: a returning player on the same device sees the game from local storage at once, and the profile, sync and economy start run in the background (`continueSession` in gate.js). A launch is about 4 requests: auth, `social_digest` (one RPC for friends, requests, unread, trades, guild, invites, challenges, notices, own profile), `sync_me` and the economy `import`, which also claims grants and deliveries, pays the stipend and brings the quest board, live ops and draws.
- Before October 2026 this was a 4 second loop that re-uploaded the whole save forever and was the main cause of server lag. Do not reintroduce per-write syncing.
- Social refresh is one `social_digest` call every 5 minutes, 15 while the live feed is up (badges are derived from it at startup and resume, then adjusted by live events), and a resume within 3 minutes of the last one skips it. Live feeds are Broadcast on private topics sent by database triggers (`live_row`, `live_read`, `live_invite_gone`): `user:<id>` (messages, reads, `seen` when you read on another device, deliveries, friendships, trades, challenges, guild invites), `guild:<id>` and `market`. The client opens one channel per topic (`src/account/feeds.js`, local bus `onLive`).
- Direct messages are announced by the sender's app, not by the insert: the insert carries `x-wikster-chat: inbox` (the `live_row` trigger on `messages` is skipped through `chat_announced()`), then `announceMessage` posts `{id, sender, recipient}` to the Realtime REST broadcast on the private topic `inbox:<recipient>` (write policy `inbox_writable`: friends only, not muted; only the owner reads it). The recipient never trusts the hint: it reads the rows by id (`confirmInbox`) and only then fires the `message` event. If the broadcast request fails, `message_live(id)` has the server send it on `user:<recipient>` as before. Inserts without the header (old apps) still go out from the trigger. The insert itself runs one guard trigger (`chat_guard`: mute, 20 a minute and 1500 a day counted from `messages_sender_idx`, no `rate_counters` write, word filter) and writes nothing else but the push queue. Staging: insert 5.05 ms mean and 4.7 KB WAL before, 2.4 ms and 0.6 KB after; delivery p95 3.8 s from the trigger, 0.2 s announced. Leaderboards and guild boards have no live feed: they refresh every 60 s while visible and on the local `score` event. Do not add tables back to the `supabase_realtime` publication; postgres_changes WAL polling was the biggest cost on the live database.
- The live project is on the Supabase Free plan (shared CPU, 500 MB RAM, small disk IO budget, 60 connections). It went down in October 2026 from write volume, not CPU. Keep writes small and rare.
- Function calls pin the region with the `forceFunctionRegion` query only (`regional(name)` in `src/account/client.js`, used by `askHouse`). Do not send the `x-region` header: functions already live reject it in CORS, and the website can land before the functions.
- The service worker (`src/sw.js`) precaches every chunk, keeps the two previous builds, and serves card pictures cache-first. A failed dynamic import reloads the page once (`src/app/errors.js`).

## Creator account and Control

- Control's SQL gives `is_admin()` accounts read and write on almost every table. `is_admin()` now also requires the `x-wikster-control: 1` header, which only Control sends. Without that, the creator's own game read everyone's rows (other players' announcements and mutes showed up as theirs).
- The game also filters announcements by live window and target, and suspensions by its own user id. Keep explicit owner filters on any query that relies on RLS alone.
- Pulling auction lots goes through `admin_cancel_auction(s)`, which returns the card and refunds the bid.
- `admin_wipe(user, 'collection' | 'everything')` runs `econ_erase` like the game's red buttons (lots, bids, trades and waiting gifts settled) and returns what it settled next to `ok`, `scope` and `log`.

## Friend codes

- No code, friend name, personal text or photo is in the repo or the client bundle. The first friend codes (the ones made before Control could) live in `redeem_codes.legacy`: one definition per code with its old id (`id`, booster look, `cards`, `extra` card with its photo URL, `theme`, `frame`, `skin`, `badge`, `message`). Redeeming one (`legacyGrant` in `src/econ/liveops.js`) writes `codesRedeemed[id]`, the claim `code:<id>` and the definition into `state.codeDefs[id]` (an `ECON_KEYS` entry), so the phone learns it only once redeemed (`src/codedefs.js`: `codeById`, `useCodeSource`, `learnCodeDefs`). Players who redeemed before the move get their definitions back on the next `snapshot` (`fillCodeDefs` in `src/econ/engine.js`, RPC `econ_code_defs`). Theme, frame and badge visuals stay in code (`code: true` on the theme or frame); ownership and the badge name come from the definition, card skins are keyed by `skin` (`[data-special="rotor"]`), and `theme_owned` / `badges_clean` read `codeDefs`. Tests use the fake codes in `tests/lib/codefixtures.mjs`; never put a real code or name in a test. The real seed is outside the repo (see CLAUDE.local.md).
- New ones are made in Control and live in `redeem_codes` with `special` (checked by `admin_special_check`, mirrored by `checkSpecial` in `src/friendcodes.js`) and `allowed` (uuid list; anyone else gets `UNKNOWN_CODE`).
- Redeeming (`redeemFromBook` in `src/econ/liveops.js`) gives the cards straight away, files one sealed booster (`{ kind: 'code', codeId: 'db:<CODE>', codeName, codeFor, accent, accent2 }`) whose exact cards are stashed in `pulls` at once (a friend booster is never drawn), owns the theme `fc-<code lowercase>` and keeps `{ name, message, cards, booster, theme, badge }` in `state.friendCodes[<CODE>]` (an `ECON_KEYS` entry). Every friend card is special and locked, keyed `special:db:<CODE>:<article key>`, keeps the rarity Control chose and its picture (`picture.source = 'upload'`), and sits in its own album.
- The theme is a base theme with the accent swapped (`composeFriend` in `src/ui/customtheme.js`); the badge (`fc-<code lowercase>`) draws an emblem from `src/data/emblems.js` in its colour. Other players see both through `profiles.appearance.friend` and `badges.earned[].look`, which `appearance_clean` and `badges_clean` rebuild from the econ state.
- Control uploads pictures to the public Storage bucket `friend-pictures` (2 MB, png, jpeg, webp, gif), writable by accounts in `public.admins` without the Control header. The schema skips the bucket where `storage` does not exist.

## Danger zone (Settings > Data)

- The three red buttons live in `src/app/danger.js`: a sheet that lists what goes and what stays, then a typed word. Offline or without the server economy they refuse and change nothing.
- Remove all cards and Erase everything are the economy `wipe` action, which calls `econ_erase` (one transaction: pulls the player's lots and refunds bidders, withdraws their bids, cancels or declines trades, drops waiting deliveries, then `econ_wipe`; `all` also leaves the guild with a handover and erases friends, messages, scores, backups and the profile and theme keys, keeping supporter perks). Lots are pulled with `admin_auction_pull` and every bid of the player is withdrawn, refunds go straight to wallets. Once a month each (`wipecards:` / `wipe:` claims). Erase keeps the player signed in. Control's wipe everything (`admin_wipe`) runs the same `econ_erase('all')`.
- Delete account is the `delete-account` function calling `delete_account(p_user)`, which settles the market and guild, keeps gifts already sent (sender set to the recipient), keeps guild bank cards (donor set null) and deletes `auth.users` in the same transaction; on a permission error it cleans up first and deletes through the auth admin API.
- Every wipe stamps `econ.state.wiped` and sends `wiped` (or `gone`) on `user:<id>`. Other devices refresh or leave at once, and a device that missed it notices the new stamp in the next economy reply. After Erase, `save_meta.wiped_at` makes `save_keys` refuse older profile and theme writes.
