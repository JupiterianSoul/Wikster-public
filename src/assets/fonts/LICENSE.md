# Bundled fonts

All fonts here are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt)
and came from the @fontsource packages.

- `comic-neue-*`: Comic Neue by the Comic Neue Project Authors, used by the Cartoon theme.
- `cinzel-*`: Cinzel by the Cinzel Project Authors.
- `bangers-*`: Bangers by Vernon Adams.
- `press-start-2p-*`: Press Start 2P by CodeMan38.
- `archivo-black-*`: Archivo Black by Omnibus-Type.
- `tilt-neon-*`: Tilt Neon by Andy Clymer.

Cinzel, Bangers, Press Start 2P, Archivo Black and Tilt Neon are used by the booster designs sold in the Atelier.
